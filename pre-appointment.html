The essentials

The five things to know first.

Allow around three hours

The tattoo itself usually takes around two to two-and-a-half hours. Your full appointment is longer because it includes numbing, consultation, mapping and careful checking.

Complete your forms before I travel

Your consultation and numbing cream forms must be returned in time for the pharmacist to review them and prepare your cream before I leave Sydney for Melbourne.

Arrive with bare brows

Please avoid makeup and skincare on the brow area. If you are coming from work or another appointment, I can help you remove it when you arrive.

Tell me about previous tattooing

Even very faded pigment can affect the technique, colour and shape available to us. Send clear photographs before the appointment if we have not already discussed it.

Your deposit secures both stages

The $1,100 package includes your first appointment in November and your six-week perfecting appointment in Melbourne on 17–19 December 2026.

Nothing starts without your approval

We draw, check and adjust the proposed shape together. Tattooing only begins once you are comfortable with the design.

The work is deliberate and collaborative. Keeping the three-hour window clear means neither of us has to hurry your mapping, colour choice or final checks.

Numbing cream forms

Your cream must be prepared before I leave for Melbourne.

Your numbing cream is specially compounded by a pharmacist using the information in your completed form. I will have every client’s cream prepared in Sydney and will bring it to Melbourne with me.

This means your form cannot be left until the appointment day. It must be returned early enough for the pharmacist to review it, contact you if they need clarification and prepare the cream before I travel. If the form is late, I may not be able to proceed with your appointment.

You do not apply it yourself

You will not need to collect, bring or apply your numbing cream. I bring it with me and apply it at Upper Skin Clinic when you arrive, then allow time for it to take effect before we begin.

Health and medications

Please tell me anything that could affect bleeding, skin or healing.

Your forms are there to keep you safe—not to decide that your medical history is “too complicated”. Include prescribed and non-prescribed medication, supplements, allergies, previous cosmetic tattooing, active skin conditions and any recent or current medical treatment.

Make sure I know about
  • Blood-thinning medication or a condition that affects bleeding or clotting
  • Current or recent cancer treatment
  • Accutane or any other medication that strongly affects the skin
  • Eczema, dermatitis, infection, broken skin or irritation around the brows
  • Previous brow tattooing, even if it now looks very faint

If you are unsure whether something matters, include it. I may ask you to speak with your doctor or obtain clearance. Never stop medication or treatment without speaking to the clinician responsible for it.

Why the preparation matters

Alcohol, caffeine and anything that increases bleeding can make the skin harder to work with and may affect how evenly pigment is placed. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours and caffeine for the three hours before your appointment. Follow your doctor’s advice—not internet advice—about prescribed medication.

What happens on the day

Nothing permanent begins until you approve the drawing.

We talk and reassess

We review your forms, skin, existing brow hair, previous tattooing, concerns and the outcome you want. I confirm the most suitable technique and answer anything that still feels unclear.

I apply your numbing cream

I apply the compounded cream I have brought from Sydney when you arrive and leave it to take effect while we talk. You may still feel mild sensation during tattooing, but the aim is to keep the appointment comfortable.

We map the design together

I draw the brows using your facial features, bone structure, natural hair and asymmetry. We sit you up, look in the mirror and adjust the drawing until it feels like you.

We document your starting point

I take before and after photographs so we can accurately compare the work and healing. They are only used publicly for the website, social media or emails with your consent.

The tattoo is built gradually

Once you approve the mapped shape, I deposit pigment within that design. I continually check the shape and symmetry, sit you upright and make small adjustments as needed.

You leave knowing exactly what comes next

I apply the aftercare product, explain the healing stages and give you written instructions. You will know what is normal, what to avoid and how to contact me if you are worried.

What to bring

Bring references that help me understand you.

You are welcome to bring photographs of brows you like, brows you definitely do not like, or photographs of yourself before your brows thinned. If your brow loss followed years of overplucking, an old photograph can be particularly helpful.

You can even bring a photograph of a close family member if you believe their natural brows show what yours may have looked like without hair loss. References help me understand your taste and history; they are never copied as a template onto your face.

Wear what feels comfortable

You will be lying down for part of the appointment and sitting up repeatedly while I check the balance. Comfortable clothing makes the three-hour visit easier.

Yes, there is a bathroom

You do not need to worry about making it through a long appointment without facilities. And yes—there will be lemon lollies, courtesy of my mum.

Your booking and second appointment

Your restoration is planned as two appointments.

Your $550 deposit secures your November appointment. The remaining $550 is paid on the day. Your six-week perfecting appointment is included in the package and takes place in Melbourne on 17–19 December 2026.

You may cancel for a refund or move your deposit until 21 October 2026. After that, the deposit is non-refundable unless I can fill your appointment. If you returned your forms on time and the screening process shows that cosmetic tattooing is not suitable for you, your deposit is returned.

Why the forms cannot wait until the day

Screening in advance protects your safety and your travel plans. It also means we are not discovering a preventable contraindication after you have arrived at the clinic.
