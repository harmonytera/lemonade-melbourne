Before your appointment

Everything to know before your brow appointment.

A little preparation makes the day calmer and gives your skin the best chance to heal well. Please read this before you come to Ivanhoe, and call me if anything is unclear.

Preparing for the day

Allow three unhurried hours.

Your appointment includes time for numbing, talking, mapping, checking the shape together and completing the tattoo. Please avoid arranging anything immediately afterwards so neither of us has to rush the decisions that matter.

Numbing cream

Unless I have given you different instructions, your numbing cream will be applied at the clinic. If I have arranged for you to apply it before arrival, follow the instructions sent directly to you. The pharmacist may contact you if they need to clarify anything in your medical form.

Health and medications

Please tell me anything that could affect your skin or healing.

Your forms are there to keep you safe, not to judge whether your medical history is “too complicated”. Please include prescribed and non-prescribed medication, previous brow tattoo, allergies, active skin conditions and any recent or current medical treatment.

Make sure I know about
  • Blood-thinning medication or a condition that affects bleeding or clotting
  • Current or recent cancer treatment
  • Accutane or other medication that strongly affects the skin
  • Eczema, dermatitis, infection, broken skin or irritation around the brows
  • Previous brow tattooing, even if it now looks very faint

If you are unsure whether something matters, include it. I may ask you to speak with your doctor or obtain clearance. Never stop medication or treatment without speaking to the clinician responsible for it.

Your booking

Your $550 deposit secures the appointment. You may cancel for a refund or move the deposit until 21 October. After that it is non-refundable unless I can fill your time. If your forms were returned on time and screening shows that tattooing is not suitable, your deposit is returned.

What happens on the day

Nothing permanent begins until you approve the drawing.

Numbing and talkingWe reassess your skin, health, concerns and the most suitable technique while the cream takes effect.
Mapping and designI draw the shape and we adjust it together in the mirror until it feels right on your face.
PhotographsI photograph your brows before and after to record the work. Public use only happens with your consent.
TattooingThe pigment is built gradually. I sit you up and reassess the balance as I work.
Before you leaveI apply aftercare, explain the healing stages and make sure you know what to do at home.

You are welcome to bring photographs of brows you like, brows you do not like, or yourself when you were younger. They help me understand your taste; they are never used as a template.
