Frequently asked questions

The things clients actually ask me.

The awkward questions belong here too. If yours isn't answered, ask it on the free call.

Ask me directly

Your brows

Will they look tattooed or too dark?

My whole method is built around avoiding that outcome. We agree on the drawn shape first, then I begin slightly softer and lighter than the final target. Your healed result tells me how to refine it safely at the second appointment.

What if I have very little or no natural hair?

You don't need existing hair for this to work. I use your bone structure, facial movement and careful mapping to build a shape that belongs on your face rather than sitting on it.

What if I don't know which style or technique suits me?

That's my job. Nano strokes, ombre powder and combination brows behave differently in different skin. I recommend what is most likely to heal well and look natural on you; the price is the same whichever we choose.

Can you work with grey or white hair?

Yes. I don't simply make the brows grey. I consider your skin undertone, the colour your brows were before they faded, what remains now and how much definition you want to live with.

What if I've been tattooed before?

Send a makeup-free photograph. Sometimes I can work over it, sometimes it needs fading first, and occasionally the honest answer is to leave it alone. I won't add another layer unless I believe I can improve it.

Can I have this after alopecia or medical treatment?

Often yes, and it is a significant part of my work. Timing, skin health and medical history matter, and I may need clearance from your doctor. If the answer is “not yet”, I'll explain what you're waiting for.

Treatment and healing

Is brow tattooing painful?

Most women describe it as scratchy and odd rather than painful. Numbing cream is applied before we begin, topped up as needed, and you can ask for a break at any point.

What does healing look like?

They are darker and sharper for the first week. Around days four to seven they flake and lighten, sometimes dramatically. Colour continues settling for four to six weeks. That temporary change is normal and is why I wait before refining them.

How long will they last?

Usually around 18 months to three years before you would want them refreshed. Skin type, sun exposure, skincare and the way your body breaks down pigment all affect longevity.

How do I know whether I'm suitable?

That's what the free call and screening forms are for. Certain skin conditions, treatments and medications affect healing or bleeding. I assess those before the appointment and will tell you plainly if I think you should not proceed.

The Melbourne visit

Where is the second appointment?

I plan to return to Melbourne in the correct six-to-eight-week window. If those dates don't suit, your included refinement can be completed at my Sydney clinic within three months.
