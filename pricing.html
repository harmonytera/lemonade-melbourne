What's included

One complete piece of work.

Each step exists to reduce the chance of you ending up with something you didn't want.

  1. Your free 15-minute call

    Before you pay me anything. Your brow loss, health, medications, previous tattoo, fears and what you actually want. If I don't think this is right for you, I'll say so here.

  2. Medical screening, properly done

    A health questionnaire and a numbing cream clearance, both assessed by me before I travel — so nothing gets discovered on the day.

  3. A full facial assessment

    Bone structure, expression, how your brows move, your asymmetry and the hair you've still got — before any shape is proposed.

  4. Mapping, drawn with you

    Up to an hour of drawing and adjusting in the mirror until you're happy. Nothing permanent starts until you say yes.

  5. Colour chosen for your colouring

    Undertone, original brow colour, what your hair is doing now, and how soft you want it to heal.

  6. Your treatment — three hours in the room

    Deliberately softer and lighter than the finished result, so there's room to build rather than regret.

  7. Aftercare, written down and on this website

    You'll know what's normal week by week, including the week you'll be convinced they're too dark. It's all here.

  8. Your refinement at six to eight weeks

    Melbourne 17–19 December, or Sydney within three months. Included, not optional, not extra.
