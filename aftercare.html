Healing and aftercare

What to expect while your brows heal.

Read this before you book if you like. There's nothing in it I wouldn't tell you on the phone, and knowing what week two looks like is half the battle.

The part nobody warns you about

They will look too dark. Then you'll think they've gone.

Not on day one — on day two or three, once the pigment has settled and before anything has flaked away. Every single client has this moment, and a fair few of them have a small private panic about it.

It passes. They flake, they lighten, and somewhere around week two you'll swear they've vanished altogether. They haven't. Colour keeps coming back up for four to six weeks. What you see at six weeks is what you actually have — and that's precisely why I refine afterwards instead of guessing beforehand.

One thing worth holding onto

The week you're most worried is usually week two, and week two is the least reliable picture of your brows you will ever see. If you're unsure about anything, ring me rather than sitting with it.
