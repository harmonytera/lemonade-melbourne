Healing and aftercare

What to expect while your brows heal.

Your brows change quite a bit before they settle. This guide explains what is normal, how to care for them, and when to contact me.

The healing stages

They may look dark first. Then much lighter.

Freshly tattooed brows usually look darker and more defined than the healed result. A little redness, swelling or tenderness can also be normal immediately afterwards.

As the surface heals, the brows can flake and look patchy. The colour may then appear unusually soft or faded before gradually returning. The result is assessed once your skin has completed its healing cycle—not during the changeable first few weeks.

Week by week

WhenWhat you may notice
Days 1–3Mild tenderness or redness, with brows looking stronger and darker than the final result.
Days 4–7Dryness and light flaking may begin. Let the skin shed naturally—do not pick or scratch it.

Weeks 4–6The colour settles and the healed shape becomes clearer.

The important bit

Do not judge your finished brows during the flaky or faded stage. If something worries you, call or message me instead of trying to fix it yourself.

Caring for them

Keep them clean, calm and left alone.

For the first 10–14 days
  • Wash your hands before touching the area.
  • Clean the brows gently with lukewarm water and a mild, fragrance-free cleanser, following the personalised instructions I give you at your appointment.
  • Pat dry with a clean tissue—do not rub.
  • Apply only a very thin layer of the recommended aftercare cream.
  • Keep makeup, exfoliants, retinoids, acids and other strong skincare away from the brow area.
  • Do not pick, peel or scratch any flakes or scabs.
  • Avoid swimming, saunas, steam and direct sun exposure for at least two weeks.
  • Do not have chemical peels or similar treatments over the area while it is healing.
Once healed

Protect the area with sunscreen. Sun exposure can make cosmetic tattoo pigment fade more quickly over time.
